The LLM was the easy part.

Building the backend for an AI advertising product, getting a model to draft ad copy was the quick part. Everything around it took the time: platform APIs with their own quirks, forecasts that depended far more on clean data than on the model, and users who only trusted automation that showed its reasoning.

Expect the model to be the smallest part of the work. Build the boring parts well.
