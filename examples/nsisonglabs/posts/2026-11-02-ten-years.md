I started Nsisong Labs in 2016. Ten years later, three lessons have held up on every project.

1 // Ship small, then learn. Every overplanned product took longer and usually solved the wrong problem.
2 // Write down what you agreed on. Two reasonable people can leave the same meeting with different memories.
3 // Measure before you rebuild. Most "scaling problems" clients brought us were an index, a query or a background job.

Still building.
