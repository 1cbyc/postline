A flaky test is a bug report nobody has read yet.

Most teams treat flakes as noise. Retry, get a green build, move on. The test is telling you something real about timing, shared state or an external call you didn't mock.

Three habits that work:

1 // Measure flake rate per test, not per build
2 // Quarantine with an owner and a deadline
3 // Never let retries hide a failure from the report

Green builds you can't trust are worse than red builds you can.
