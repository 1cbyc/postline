We cut a customer's CI from 22 minutes to 6 without touching their hardware.

Nothing clever. We measured where the time went and found most of it was work the pipeline had already done on a previous run.

→ Remote build cache: 71% of steps skipped on a typical PR
→ Tests sharded by historical timing instead of file count
→ Docker layers ordered so dependency installs stay cached

The full write-up, with the config: northwind.dev/blog/ci-22-to-6
